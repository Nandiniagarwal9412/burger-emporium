THE EMPORIUM STORY

BORN FROM A BIG CRAVING.

Burger Emporium started with a craving for something bigger, bolder and more exciting than an ordinary burger.

Instead of following the usual recipe, we decided to create our own. Indian spices, crispy patties, creamy sauces, fresh vegetables and seriously satisfying buns all came together under one roof.

The result? Big burgers. Desi flavours. Zero boring bites.
